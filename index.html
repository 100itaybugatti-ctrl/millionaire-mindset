Budgeting

Give your money a plan. Track what comes in, what goes out, and what you want to set aside.

◈

Saving

▤

Useful skills

◎

Smart decisions

Compare options, check reliable sources, understand risks, and avoid decisions made under pressure.

◇

A practical path

Your wealth-building roadmap

Use these steps as a learning guide. Adjust them to your age, situation, and responsibilities.

STEP 01

Understand

Learn how income, spending, saving, and needs differ. Start by observing your habits.

STEP 02

Plan

Set a realistic goal and make a simple plan that leaves room for essentials and flexibility.

STEP 03

Develop

Practice useful skills, ask for feedback, and keep learning from trustworthy sources.

STEP 04

Review

Check your progress regularly. Learn from what worked and update what did not.
